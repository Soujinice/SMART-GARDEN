Safety checklist

  • Never connect HC-SR04 ECHO straight to the Pi - always use the divider.
  • DHT11 goes on 3.3V (Pin 1), not 5V.
  • Use one shared ground; never short 5V (Pins 2/4) to any GPIO.
  • Keep the tank, pump and tubing below and away from the Pi and breadboard.
  • Mount the ultrasonic sensor on the tank lid, at least 3 cm above the full water line.
